# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Vastora community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting
differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is
best for the community. Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing
private information, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered
inappropriate is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
Report concerns privately through the project's security reporting channel.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
